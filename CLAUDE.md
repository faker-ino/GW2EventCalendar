## What this is

GW2EventCalendar is a [Nexus](https://github.com/RaidcoreGG/Nexus) addon (a Windows x64 DLL, not a
standalone executable) for Guild Wars 2 that shows a WoW-Calendar-style month grid of events pulled from
the official GW2 forum's Events calendar (`https://en-forum.guildwars2.com/events/`).
